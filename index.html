Positive phototropism: the coleoptile bends toward unilateral light. Auxin (IAA) made at the tip migrates to the shaded side, which elongates more than the lit side, pushing the tip toward the light — the classic Cholodny-Went mechanism from Darwin's and Went's coleoptile experiments. The wall keeps each pot lit by only its own sun, so the two demonstrations don't interfere.
