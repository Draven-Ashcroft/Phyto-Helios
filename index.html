Positive Phototropism (Cholodny–Went Mechanism)

Auxin (IAA) is produced at the coleoptile tip. When light strikes from one side, auxin redistributes to the shaded side, where it promotes greater cell elongation. This unequal growth causes the shoot to bend towards the light.
